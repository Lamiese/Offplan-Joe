Golden Visa

UAE residency through property investment

A 10-year renewable UAE residency visa for you and your family, through eligible off-plan or ready property.

How It Works

The essentials

AED 2M minimum

Invest AED 2 million or more in eligible UAE property to qualify for the Golden Visa route.

10-year renewable

The visa is valid for 10 years and renewable, with no local sponsor required.

Family included

Sponsor your spouse and children under the same Golden Visa application.

Off-plan eligible

Many off-plan purchases from approved developers qualify, subject to payment terms.

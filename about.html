About Me

Ten years building wealth for investors in Dubai

The story behind Off Plan Joe — and the philosophy that drives every recommendation.

Joe Prior has called Dubai home for the past ten years, establishing himself as a leading expert in off-plan real estate.

With a proven track record of success, Joe has sold projects ranging from AED 1 million to AED 50 million, building strong investment portfolios and lasting wealth for his clients.

Driven by integrity and transparency, Joe's approach is simple: recommend only the projects that truly align with his clients' goals. Over the past ten years, he has achieved more than AED 500 million in closed sales and earned recognition as a top-performing broker within some of Dubai's most prestigious real estate firms.

Today, Joe is on a mission to help investors create exponential wealth in one of the world's most dynamic and fast-evolving cities, Dubai.
